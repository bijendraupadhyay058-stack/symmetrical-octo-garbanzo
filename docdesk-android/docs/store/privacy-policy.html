Summary

DocDesk processes your documents on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your documents or personal data. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

Documents and files

  • Files you open are read only with your permission, through Android's file picker or "Share/Open with".
  • Conversions, OCR (text recognition), editing, signing, redaction and all other tools run on your phone. Documents are never sent to us or to third parties.
  • Results are stored in the app's private storage ("My Files") or wherever you choose to save them. Temporary files are deleted after use and automatically within a day.
  • App files are excluded from cloud backups.

Passwords and certificates

PDF passwords and certificate passwords are used only in memory while you work and are never stored or transmitted. Digital-signature certificates you import or create, and signatures you draw, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

History

The optional processing history stores only file names, dates and results on your device. You can turn it off or clear it in Settings.

Problem reports

If the app crashes, technical details (app and Android version, phone model, error trace) are saved on your device with file names, paths and e-mail addresses removed. Nothing is sent automatically. You may choose to share a report using your own e-mail or messaging app.

Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (bundled in the app) recognises text on the device; images are not sent to Google.
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner is provided by Google Play services. Google Play services may download the scanner module the first time you use it. Scanned images are returned to DocDesk on your device. Google's handling of Play services is described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Text-to-speech (Read aloud) uses your phone's installed speech engine.

Permissions

DocDesk does not request access to all of your storage, contacts or location. The camera is used only through the document scanner when you choose to scan. Fingerprint/face/screen lock is used only if you turn on App lock.

Children

DocDesk is a business productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.
